Our Story

A live bakery and cafe built around craft & comfort.

Theatre is designed as a stage for baking. Our bakers work in an open, live counter — kneading, shaping and pulling fresh breads from the oven through the day, while our cafe serves coffee, breakfast plates and desserts that pair beautifully with what's baking.

Whether you're stopping by for a morning coffee, picking up a fresh loaf, or settling in for an unhurried meal — Theatre is a place where every visit feels like a small performance.
